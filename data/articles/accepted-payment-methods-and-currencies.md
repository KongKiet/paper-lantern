OptiSigns bills in five currencies: US dollars, euros, British pounds, Canadian dollars and Australian dollars. Every currency accepts credit and debit cards. US-dollar accounts can also pay from a US bank account, and euro accounts can pay by SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL | Wero or Bancontact. You choose your currency when you first subscribe. To change it later, contact support.

<a name="PaymentMethodsByCurrency"></a>

## Payment Methods by Currency

The payment options you see depend on the currency your account is billed in:

- **US dollars (USD)** - Card or US bank account.
- **Euros (EUR)** - Card, iDEAL | Wero, SEPA Direct Debit or Bancontact.
- **British pounds (GBP)** - Card only.
- **Canadian dollars (CAD)** - Card only.
- **Australian dollars (AUD)** - Card only.

Cards are the quickest option: the payment goes through as soon as you subscribe. Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover are accepted.

A few kinds of billing are card only, whatever the currency:

- **Sub-accounts** - A sub-account's subscription, paid from the parent account's **Sub-Account Billing** page.
- **Teams with their own billing** - A team that pays for its own screens.
- **Hardware orders** - Buying an OptiSigns player or other device from the portal.

## Choose Your Billing Currency

You choose your currency in the subscription wizard, while your account is on a free trial or the Free plan.

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Update subscription**. On **Choose the best plan for you**, open the currency menu at the top right and choose your currency.

![Subscription wizard plan step with the currency menu open, listing USD, EUR, GBP, CAD and AUD](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57703880204307)

Choose your plan and click **Next**. Set your number of screens and billing term, then continue to **Payment**. The payment options shown are the ones for the currency you picked.

Your first paid subscription sets your billing currency. After that, the wizard shows your currency as plain text with an information icon instead of a menu: "Your billing currency is set by your subscription and cannot be changed. To change it, please contact support." Teams with their own billing always use the main account's currency.

## Add or Change Your Payment Method

You add a payment method on the **Payment** step when you subscribe. To change it later, go to **Payment Settings**, then in **Billing Information** click **Update Payment Method**. The full steps, and what happens to an unpaid invoice when you save, are in [Billing Details, Failed Payments & Receipts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014).

Only the account **Owner**, a **Super Admin** or an **Admin** can change the payment method.

### US Dollars: Card or US Bank Account

Choose **Card** to enter your card number, expiration date, security code and ZIP code.

![Update Payment Method form in Payment Settings showing the Card and US bank account options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57703865144979)

Choose **US bank account** to pay from a US checking or savings account. Search for your bank and sign in to it to connect the account.

### Canadian Dollars: Card

Accounts billed in Canadian dollars pay by credit or debit card, so the payment form shows the card fields only.

Paying from a bank account isn't available in Canadian dollars. The US bank account option is offered only to accounts billed in US dollars, because those bank payments can only be made in US dollars. If you'd rather pay by invoice, see [Pay by Invoice, Purchase Order or Check](#PayByInvoicePurchaseOrderOrCheck).

### Euros: Card, iDEAL | Wero, SEPA Direct Debit or Bancontact

Euro accounts see four options on the payment form. Non-card methods also ask for your full name, country and billing address.

Choose **SEPA Debit** to pay directly from a euro bank account. Enter your **IBAN**, and OptiSigns collects each payment from that account.

![Euro payment form with the SEPA Debit option selected and the IBAN field](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57703865145107)

Choose **iDEAL | Wero** or **Bancontact** to approve the setup in your own bank. After you click **Update subscription** you're taken to your bank's secure page. Once you approve, future payments are collected from the same bank account by direct debit.

![Euro payment form with the iDEAL | Wero option selected and a notice that you'll be redirected to complete the next steps](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57703865146259)

### British Pounds and Australian Dollars: Card

Accounts billed in pounds or Australian dollars pay by card, so the payment form shows the card fields only.

![Card-only payment form with card number, expiration date, security code, country and ZIP code](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57703880206611)

## How Bank Payments Work

Card payments are confirmed straight away. Bank payments take longer:

- **US bank account** - A payment usually takes about a week to clear.
- **SEPA Direct Debit** - A payment can take up to about two weeks to clear. You're notified before each debit, and you can ask your bank to refund a debit within 8 weeks.
- **iDEAL | Wero and Bancontact** - You approve the setup in your bank right away. Later payments are collected by direct debit, so they clear in the same way as SEPA Direct Debit.

If the bank rejects a payment while it's clearing, for example because of insufficient funds, the invoice becomes unpaid and you'll see it on your billing pages. To fix it, see [Billing Details, Failed Payments & Receipts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014).

<a name="PayByInvoicePurchaseOrderOrCheck"></a>

## Pay by Invoice, Purchase Order or Check

If your organization needs to pay by purchase order, bank transfer or check, or on invoice terms, this isn't set up in the portal. Email our billing team at [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) with the plan you want, how many screens you need, your billing address, and any hardware you'd like with a shipping address. They'll send you a quote and set up invoice billing for your account. Payments by check carry a $25 processing fee.

<a name="TroubleshootingAndFAQ"></a>

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common issues our customers may face when choosing a payment method or currency, and how to solve them.

#### I Don't See SEPA, iDEAL or Bancontact

These options appear only for accounts billed in euros. Check the currency shown on **Choose the best plan for you** in the subscription wizard. If you're still on a trial or the Free plan, switch the currency menu to **EUR €**. Sub-account, team and hardware payments are card only, so bank options never appear there.

#### I Don't See the US Bank Account Option

US bank accounts are offered to accounts billed in US dollars. Sub-account, team and hardware payments are card only.

#### Can I Pay With Sofort?

No. Sofort is no longer offered. Euro accounts can use SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL | Wero, Bancontact or a card instead.

#### I Can't Change My Currency

Your account already has a paid subscription, so you can't change the currency yourself. Contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com). They can switch it for you, and the new currency applies from your next billing period.

#### "Payment Was Not Completed. Please Try Again or Use a Different Payment Method."

You left your bank's page before approving, or the bank didn't confirm. Nothing was set up. Click **Update subscription** again and approve in your bank, or choose a different payment method.

#### My Bank Asks Me to Approve a Payment

Some banks ask you to confirm a payment, for example in your banking app. Click **Complete payment** on the banner or in **Billing History**. See [Billing Details, Failed Payments & Receipts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014).

#### Does Changing My Payment Method Change My Plan?

No. Changing your payment method doesn't change your plan, screens or billing date. To change those, see [Billing: Changing Plans or Screen Counts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500000493782). To stop paying, see [Cancel, Pause or Downgrade Your Subscription](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/17639078588691).
