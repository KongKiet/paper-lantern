You can change your plan, add or remove screens, and switch between Monthly and Yearly billing at any time from **Subscription Plan** in your account settings. Click **Update subscription**, make your changes, and confirm on the payment step. Adding screens or moving to a higher plan is charged right away for the rest of your current billing period; removing screens or moving to a lower plan turns the unused time into account credit.

---

<a name="BeforeYouStart"></a>

## Before You Start

- **Who can change billing** - Only the account **Owner**, or a **Super Admin** or **Admin** of the main account, can see **Subscription Plan** and change the subscription. Other users don't see the billing pages at all.
- **Your currency is set at your first purchase** - On a trial or Free Plan you pick the currency you pay in (US dollars, euros, British pounds, Canadian dollars or Australian dollars). Once you subscribe, the currency is locked. To change it, contact our support team.
- **Accounts with special pricing** - If your subscription is managed by our billing team, the **Update subscription** button isn't shown and you'll see a message asking you to contact support. Our team will make the change for you. If you have custom contract pricing, you can change your number of screens at your contract rate, but not your plan.
- **Sub-accounts and Team Billing** - A sub-account, and a team with its own billing, can only change its number of screens. The plan is set by the main account. See [How to Manage Sub-Account Billing with OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/17129313173395-How-to-manage-Sub-Account-billing-with-OptiSigns).

<a name="SubscribeFromATrialOrFreePlan"></a>

## Subscribe From a Trial or Free Plan

Open the account menu at the top right and choose **Subscription Plan**, then click **Update subscription**.

Choose your currency from the menu above the plans. Choose carefully, since it can't be changed later without contacting support.

![Plan cards on a trial with the currency menu open, listing USD, EUR, GBP, CAD and AUD](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614154167443)

Pick a plan: **Standard**, **Pro Plus** or **Engage**. Click **Select** on the plan you want, then **Next**.

Choose **Yearly** or **Monthly** billing. Yearly saves 10%.

Set the number of screens with the **-** and **+** buttons. The total updates as you go. Then click **Continue to payment**.

Enter your payment details and review the **Account Summary**. **Due Today** is what you'll be charged now, including any sales tax.

Click **Update subscription** to confirm.

## Change Your Plan or Screens on a Paid Subscription

When you already have a paid subscription, **Update subscription** takes you straight to your screen count, since that's the change most people make. The plans are one step back.

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Update subscription**.

![Subscription Plan page with the plan summary and the Update subscription button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614147015443)

To add or remove screens, use the **-** and **+** buttons under **Number of screens**. The line above the counter shows how many screens are paired and how many licenses you have now.

To switch billing, choose **Yearly** or **Monthly** at the top.

![Screen count step with Yearly and Monthly billing options and the screen counter highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614154170643)

To change your plan, click **Back to all plans**, click **Select** on the plan you want, then **Next**. You'll return to the screen count step.

![Plan cards for Standard and Pro Plus with the Pro Plus Select button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614154172051)

Click **Continue to payment**. Check the **Account Summary**: it lists your plan, each add-on and the amount **Due Today**.

Click **Update subscription** to confirm the change.

![Account Summary on the payment step with the Update subscription button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614154172819)

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| You can't reduce your licenses below the number of screens in use, including screens reserved for other teams. If you try, OptiSigns asks you to move screens to OnHold or remove them first. Moving a screen to OnHold frees its license, but it doesn't lower your bill until you also reduce the screen count here. See [About the OnHold Devices Folder](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003244381-About-the-OnHold-Devices-Folder). |

Moving to a lower plan can also mean removing things the lower plan doesn't include, such as extra users, teams or share links. OptiSigns lists anything you need to remove before the change can go through.

<a name="HowChargesWork"></a>

## How Charges Work

Every change is prorated: you pay, or get credit, only for the time left in your current billing period.

- **Adding screens** - Charged right away for the rest of the current billing period. For example, on Standard Monthly at $10 per screen, adding one screen halfway through the month costs about $5 today, and the full $10 from your next bill.
- **Removing screens** - Takes effect right away. The unused time on the removed screens is added to your account as credit and used toward your upcoming invoices.
- **Moving to a higher plan** - Charged right away for the price difference over the rest of your billing period.
- **Moving to a lower paid plan** - Takes effect right away. The unused value of your old plan becomes account credit for upcoming invoices.
- **Switching from Monthly to Yearly** - Charged right away. Any unused value of your current monthly period is credited against the yearly price.
- **Switching from Yearly to Monthly** - Takes effect right away. The unused part of your yearly payment becomes account credit, which is used toward your monthly invoices.
- **Downgrading to the Free Plan** - Takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then, your paid features keep working, and the **Subscription Plan** page shows the date your plan will switch.

**Due Today** on the payment step always shows what will be charged now, including tax. After you confirm, the receipt shows the itemized charges and any credit applied. You'll find all your invoices under **Billing History**.

### Add-Ons

Add-ons such as Video Wall, AeriCast, OptiSound and OptiDev are listed under **Account Add-Ons** on the **Subscription Plan** page, where you can add or change them. Add-ons are billed on the same invoice as your plan and follow the same billing period: if you switch between Monthly and Yearly, your add-ons switch too. Adding add-on licenses is prorated the same way as adding screens.

![Account Add-Ons section of the Subscription Plan page, listing Video Wall, OptiSound Music, AeriCast, OptiDev, Custom Domain and X Social](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57614147021587)

If you upgrade to the Engage plan while you have the Engage add-on, the add-on is removed automatically and its unused time is credited on the same invoice, since Engage features are included in the plan.

For details on specific add-ons, see [Engage Plan - FAQs](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/23565267463315-Engage-Plan-FAQs) and [OptiDev Add-on - Free Trial and Pricing](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/54095075680275-OptiDev-Add-on-Free-Trial-and-Pricing).

<a name="TroubleshootingAndFAQ"></a>

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common issues our customers may face when changing their plan or screen count, and how to solve them.

#### I Don't See Subscription Plan or the Update Subscription Button

Only the account Owner, or a Super Admin or Admin of the main account, can change billing. Ask one of them to make the change. If you see "Your subscription is managed by our billing team. Please contact support to change it.", your account has special pricing, so contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com) and we'll update it for you.

#### I Can't Change My Currency

Your currency is set when you first subscribe and can't be changed in the portal. Contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com) if you need to pay in a different currency.

#### I Can't Reduce My Number of Screens

You can't go below the number of screens in use. Move the screens you no longer need to the OnHold Devices folder, or remove them, then reduce the count. See [About the OnHold Devices Folder](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003244381-About-the-OnHold-Devices-Folder).

#### Can I Pay by Invoice or Purchase Order?

Contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com) to ask about paying by invoice or purchase order. To see which payment methods you can use online, see [Accepted Payment Methods](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/34379983350163-Accepted-Payment-Methods).

#### Do You Offer a Non-Profit or Education Discount?

See [Does OptiSigns Offer a Non-profit/Education Discount?](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016252654-Does-OptiSigns-offer-Non-profit-Education-discount)

#### How Do I Cancel My Subscription?

Click **Cancel Subscription** on the screen count step of **Update subscription**. Your subscription stays active until the end of your billing period. See [How to Cancel or Pause Your Subscription](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/17639078588691-How-to-cancel-subscription-or-pause-subscription-for-Seasonal-Use-Case).
