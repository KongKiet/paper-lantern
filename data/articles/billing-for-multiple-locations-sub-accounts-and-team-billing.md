If you run OptiSigns across several locations, franchises or departments, you can decide who pays for which screens. Each location can have its own OptiSigns account that you manage from yours (Sub-Account Billing), or its own bill inside your one account (Team Billing). You can also keep one bill and simply split your screens between teams.

---

<a name="WhichOptionIsRightForYou"></a>

## Which Option Is Right for You

There are three ways to set up OptiSigns billing for several locations. They differ in two things: whether each location gets its own OptiSigns account, and who pays the bill.

#### Sub-Account Billing

**Each location has its own account.** Choose this when each location, such as a franchise, should have its own OptiSigns sign-in, screens, content and users. Each location has its own subscription, and you can manage and pay its bills from your account. It's available on any paid plan. See [Set Up Sub-Account Billing](#SetUpSubAccountBilling).

#### Team Billing

**One account, a separate bill for each location.** Choose this when you want to manage every location from your own account, but each location pays for its own screens. Each location is a team with its own subscription and invoices, on the same plan as your account. It's available on Pro Plus and above, and our billing team turns it on for you. See [Team Billing](#TeamBilling).

#### Teams on One Bill

**One account, one bill.** Choose this when you pay for everything yourself and only need to keep each location's screens and content apart. Create a team for each location and set its **Number of screens limit** to reserve screens for it. It's available on any plan. See [Working with Teams and Security Levels](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034883113-Working-with-Teams-and-Security-Levels).

## Set Up Sub-Account Billing

Sub-Account Billing lets your main account (the parent) look after the billing of other OptiSigns accounts (the sub-accounts). Each sub-account keeps its own screens, content and users; you only manage its subscription and payments.

- **Who can set it up** - The account **Owner**, a **Super Admin** or an **Admin** of the main account.
- **Plan** - Any paid plan. On a Free Plan, the page asks you to upgrade first.
- **Not available for** - Branded portal (white-label) accounts, and accounts that are already a sub-account of someone else.

### Activate Sub-Account Billing

Open the settings menu and, under **Billing & Orders**, click **Sub-Account Billing**. You can also open the account menu at the top right and choose **More**, then **Sub-Account Billing**.

![Settings menu with the Billing & Orders group open and Sub-Account Billing highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827450899)

Click **Activate**, then click **Yes** to confirm.

![Sub-Account Billing page before setup, with the intro text and the Activate button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827452947)

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| Once Sub-Account Billing is activated, it can't be turned off. Your account also can't become a sub-account of another account after that. |

### Add a Sub-Account

Click **Add Sub-Account**. You can link an OptiSigns account that already exists, or create a new one.

![Add Sub-Account dialog with the Link Account and Create New Account buttons](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827453843)

#### Link an Existing Account

Click **Link Account** and enter the **Owner username/email** of the account you want to add. It must be that account's Owner, not another user. Click **Link Account**.

![Link Account dialog with the Owner username/email field](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827454483)

The account's Owner gets an email and a notification in OptiSigns. When they open it, they'll see a **Sub-Account billing request** and click **Confirm**. Until then, the account shows as **Invited** in your list. To withdraw the request, click the invited account and choose **Remove Invite**.

Linking doesn't change the account's subscription. It keeps its plan, currency, payment method and invoices; you simply gain access to manage them.

#### Create a New Account

Click **Create New Account** and fill in:

- **Billing email** - Where the new account's invoices and receipts go.
- **First admin username/email** and **First admin password** - The sign-in for the new account's first admin. You can let OptiSigns generate the password, and ask them to change it at their next sign-in.
- **Number of screens** - How many screen licenses the new account starts with.
- **Send credentials to** - Optional. An email address to send the new sign-in details to.
- **Currency** and **Plan** - Copied from your account. They can't be changed here.
- **Payment** - Choose **Automatically charge this payment method on file** and enter a card, or **Email invoice to** the billing email. An emailed invoice is due within 30 days.
- **Memo** - Optional. A purchase order number or payment instructions to show on the invoice.

![Create New Account dialog with Email invoice to selected, plus the Country and Memo fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827458323)

Click **Create Sub-account**. You'll see the new account's sign-in details, with buttons to copy them.

<a name="ManageASubAccount"></a>

## Manage a Sub-Account

Your sub-accounts are listed on **Sub-Account Billing**, with each one's screens, plan, next invoice date and total. Click a sub-account to manage it. It has three tabs:

- **Subscription Plan** - Click **Update subscription** to add or remove screens. You can change the number of screens only, not the plan.
- **Payment Settings** - The sub-account's payment method, billing email and billing address. The payment methods offered depend on its currency, as for any account.
- **Billing History** - The sub-account's invoices and receipts.

![A sub-account's page with the Subscription Plan, Payment Settings and Billing History tabs](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827460115)

To rename a sub-account, hover over its name at the top of the page and click the pencil icon.

If a sub-account has an unpaid invoice, a banner asks you to complete the payment or update its payment method. You can't change its screens until the invoice is paid. See [Billing Details, Failed Payments & Receipts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014).

### What the Sub-Account Sees

A sub-account's own Owner and Admins still see its **Subscription Plan**, **Payment Settings** and **Billing History**, and they can change its number of screens. They can't change its plan. Screens, content and users stay completely separate from yours.

### Remove a Sub-Account

On the sub-account's page, click **Remove**, then confirm. The account isn't deleted: it becomes a standalone account again, and its subscription keeps running on its own payment method. You just lose access to its billing. To manage it again, send a new link request.

<a name="TeamBilling"></a>

## Team Billing

Team Billing gives a team inside your account its own subscription, payment method and invoices. Each location's team admins look after their own bill, while you keep every screen and team in one account.

- **Availability** - Pro Plus and above. Team Billing is turned on by our billing team: email [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) to ask for it.
- **Who can set it up** - The account **Owner** or a **Super Admin**.

### Turn On Billing for a Team

Go to **Account Members**. To create a team with its own billing, click **Add Team**; to give an existing team its own billing, click the team's **⋮** menu and choose **Update Team**. Turn on **Enable team based billing**.

![Add Team dialog with Enable team based billing turned on, followed by the billing email and first admin fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729827460499)

Fill in the team's **Billing email**, its **First admin username/email**, which becomes the team's Owner, the **Currency** and the team's **Number of screens limit**, which is the number of screens the team pays for. The plan is always your account's plan. Choose to charge a card now, or to email the billing contact so they can add a payment method, and save.

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| Billing can't be turned off for a team once it's on, and the team's currency can't be changed later. |

### Manage a Team's Billing

On **Account Members**, click **Team Billing** on the team's row. The team's Owner and Admins can open it too.

![A team's row on Account Members with the Team Billing button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57729836623507)

A team's billing page has the same three tabs as a sub-account: **Subscription Plan** (number of screens only), **Payment Settings** (card only) and **Billing History**. Add-ons stay on your main account. If you change your account's plan, the change applies to every billing team.

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common issues our customers may face when setting up billing for multiple locations, and how to solve them.

#### I Don't See Sub-Account Billing

Only the account Owner, a Super Admin or an Admin of a main account can see it. It isn't available on branded portal accounts, or on an account that is itself a sub-account. On a Free Plan, the page asks you to upgrade.

#### "Username or Email Not Found"

Enter the username or email of the other account's **Owner**. Another user's details won't work, even if they're an admin.

#### "This Account Is Already a Sub Account of Another Account"

An account can only have one parent. Ask the other parent to remove it first.

#### "Unable to Link to This Account as a Sub-Account"

The account you entered has Sub-Account Billing activated itself, so it can't become a sub-account. It's also shown if you enter your own account.

#### Why Can't I Change a Sub-Account's Plan?

New sub-accounts take your plan, and you can only change their number of screens. For a different plan, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).

#### Can I Pay a Sub-Account's Invoices by Purchase Order?

Yes. When you create the sub-account, choose **Email invoice to** and put your PO number in **Memo**. For anything else about invoices, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).

#### I Don't See Enable Team Based Billing

Team Billing needs to be turned on for your account first, and only the Owner or a Super Admin sees the toggle. It isn't available on a Free Plan or during a trial. Email [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) to ask for it.
