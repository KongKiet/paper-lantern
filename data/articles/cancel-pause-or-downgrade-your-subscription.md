### In this article, we'll show you how to pause OptiSigns for the off season, move to the Free plan, or cancel your subscription, and how to undo a cancellation before it takes effect.

- [Before You Start](#BeforeYouStart)
- [Pause for Seasonal Use](#PauseForSeasonalUse)
- [Downgrade to the Free Plan](#DowngradeToTheFreePlan)
- [Cancel Your Subscription](#CancelYourSubscription)
- [Troubleshooting and FAQ](#TroubleshootingAndFAQ)

You can change your subscription at any time from **Subscription Plan** in your account settings. To **pause** for the off season, move the screens you won't use to **OnHold Devices** and lower your number of screens, so you pay only for the screens you keep. To **downgrade**, switch to the Free plan and keep your account with up to 3 screens. To **cancel**, click **Cancel Subscription**: your plan stays active until the end of your billing period, and you can undo it until then.

---

<a name="BeforeYouStart"></a>

## Before You Start

- **Who can do it** - Only the account **Owner** or a **Super Admin** can cancel or resume a subscription. Other users don't see the billing pages, or can't make these changes. Ask one of them.
- **Where to find it** - Click your profile icon at the top right of the portal and choose **Subscription Plan**. Then click **Update subscription**.

![Subscription Plan page with the screen count, plan price and the Update subscription button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443429139)

- **Your content is kept** - Cancelling or downgrading doesn't delete your files, playlists or screens. If you subscribe again later, they're still there.
- **Changes take effect at the end of your billing period** - Both a cancellation and a downgrade to Free are scheduled for the end of the period you've already paid for. Until then, everything keeps working.
- **If you don't see Cancel Subscription** - You may be on a free trial or the Free plan, which have nothing to cancel, or your subscription may be managed by our billing team. In that case, contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com).

<a name="PauseForSeasonalUse"></a>

## Pause for Seasonal Use

OptiSigns doesn't have a pause button. If you only need your screens part of the year, lower your number of screens instead of cancelling. You keep your account, your content and your settings, and you pay only for the screens you keep.

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Update subscription**. Under **Number of screens**, click **−** to lower the count. The price on the right updates as you go.

![Number of screens with 2 screens paired and 3 current licenses, the minus button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619488988691)

You can't go below the number of screens you have paired. When you reach that number, clicking **−** opens **Screen Management**: "Move screens to OnHold or remove them to match your subscription."

Click the pause button next to a screen to move it to **OnHold Devices**, or the bin to unpair it. A screen in OnHold keeps its settings but doesn't use a license, and it shows blank content until you move it back.

![Screen Management dialog listing two paired screens, with the Move to OnHold button highlighted on the first](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443430931)

Then lower the number of screens and click **Continue to payment** to confirm the change. You get a credit for the unused time on the screens you removed, and it's applied to your next invoice.

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| Moving a screen to OnHold on its own doesn't lower your bill. You also need to lower your number of screens. |

When the season starts again, raise your number of screens on the same page, then move your screens back out of **OnHold Devices**. Adding screens is charged straight away, for the rest of the current billing period. For more about OnHold, see [About the OnHold Devices Folder](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003244381).

<a name="DowngradeToTheFreePlan"></a>

## Downgrade to the Free Plan

The Free plan keeps your account open at no cost, with up to 3 screens and fewer features. See [What Do I Get With an OptiSigns Free Plan?](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/33940834613139) for what's included.

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Update subscription**. Click **Back to all plans**. Under the plan cards, next to "Don't want to keep your current features?", click **Downgrade to Free Plan**.

![Bottom of the plan chooser with the Downgrade to Free Plan link highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443431571)

Choose the reason you're downgrading. You can add a comment in **Tell us more about your decision**. Click **Downgrade to free**.

![Why are you downgrading survey with a reason selected and the Downgrade to free button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443432211)

The next screen compares your plan with the Free plan. Click **Yes, downgrade to free plan**.

![Comparison of the current plan and the Free plan, with the Yes, downgrade to free plan button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443432723)

Finally, click **Confirm Downgrade**. The Free plan supports up to 3 screens, on OptiSigns, Windows, Mac, Linux or Raspberry Pi players. If you have more than that, only the latest 3 supported screens stay active at the end of the period, and the rest move to **OnHold Devices**. To choose which screens stay active, move the others to OnHold yourself before the date.

**Subscription Plan** now shows "Your plan will switch to the Free Plan on [date]". You keep your current plan until then. To stay on your paid plan, click **Resume Subscription** before that date.

![Subscription Plan showing Your plan will switch to the Free Plan on a date, with the Resume Subscription button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443438227)

<a name="CancelYourSubscription"></a>

## Cancel Your Subscription

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Update subscription**. Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom left.

![Plan details with the Cancel Subscription link highlighted at the bottom left](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619489002771)

Tell us why you're cancelling, then click **Continue cancellation**. If you change your mind here, click **Keep current plan**.

![Why are you cancelling survey with a reason selected and the Continue cancellation button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443442067)

Depending on your reason, we may show you another option first. For example, if you only need OptiSigns part of the year, you'll see "Only need OptiSigns part time?". Click **Pause your devices instead** to learn about OnHold Devices, or tell us how you use OptiSigns and click **Send to OptiSigns team**. To go on cancelling, click **No thanks, continue cancellation**.

![Only need OptiSigns part time dialog with the Pause your devices instead option highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443444115)

You'll see "Are you sure you want to cancel your subscription?" with the date your subscription stays active until. Click **Cancel subscription** to confirm, or **Keep my current subscription** to go back.

![Cancel confirmation with the date the subscription stays active until and the Cancel subscription button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619489005843)

**Subscription Plan** now shows "Your subscription is scheduled to end on [date]". Your plan, screens and add-ons keep working until then, and you won't be charged again.

<a name="ChangeYourMindResumeYourSubscription"></a>

### Change Your Mind: Resume Your Subscription

Until the end date, you can undo the cancellation. Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Resume Subscription**.

![Your subscription is scheduled to end on a date, with the Resume Subscription button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619489015955)

Click **Resume** to confirm. You'll see "Subscription resumed", and your subscription renews and is charged as usual at the start of your next billing period.

![Resume Subscription confirmation with the Resume button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57619443447443)

The same **Resume Subscription** button also undoes a scheduled downgrade to the Free plan.

### What Happens When Your Subscription Ends

On the end date, billing stops and your account becomes inactive. Your screens move to **OnHold Devices** and show a "Your Device is On Hold" message instead of your content. Your content is kept.

When you sign in, you'll see "Your subscription needs attention" and "Your content is safe." To use OptiSigns again, click **Update Payment**, choose a plan and your number of screens, and subscribe. Then move your screens back out of **OnHold Devices**.

<a name="TroubleshootingAndFAQ"></a>

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common questions our customers have about cancelling, pausing or downgrading, and how to solve them.

#### Can I Get a Refund?

Cancelling doesn't refund the rest of your current billing period: your plan stays active until the end date instead. When you lower your number of screens, you get a credit for the unused time on those screens, applied to your next invoice. To ask about a refund, for example on a yearly plan, contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com).

For the full terms, see the [OptiSigns Terms of Use](https://www.optisigns.com/terms-of-use).

#### I Can't Lower My Number of Screens

You can't go below the number of screens you have paired. Move the screens you don't need to **OnHold Devices**, or unpair them, then lower the count. See [Pause for Seasonal Use](#PauseForSeasonalUse).

#### Can I Lower My Screens to Zero?

No. To stop paying altogether, cancel your subscription, or downgrade to the Free plan to keep your account at no cost.

#### I Don't See the Cancel Subscription Button

Cancel Subscription is shown only on a paid plan, after you click **Update subscription** on **Subscription Plan**. It isn't shown during a free trial, on the Free plan, or when our billing team manages your subscription. Only the account Owner or a Super Admin can cancel. If none of these apply, contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com).

#### When Does My Cancellation Take Effect?

At the end of your current billing period: the end of the month for a monthly plan, or of the year for a yearly plan. The date is shown on **Subscription Plan**.

#### I Cancelled by Mistake

Go to **Subscription Plan** and click **Resume Subscription** before the end date. See [Change Your Mind: Resume Your Subscription](#ChangeYourMindResumeYourSubscription).

#### Should I Delete My Account Instead?

Only if you want to remove everything for good. Deleting your account cancels your subscription immediately, with no credit for the time left, and removes your account. See [How to Delete Your Account from OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404608957075).

#### How Do I Change My Plan or Add Screens?

See [Billing: Changing Plans or Screen Counts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500000493782). For payment and invoice questions, see [Billing Details, Failed Payments & Receipts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014).
