Your billing details live under **Billing & Orders** in your account settings. **Payment Settings** holds your billing email, payment method, billing address and tax ID. **Billing History** lists your invoices and receipts. If a payment fails, update your payment method in **Payment Settings**: saving a new card pays your outstanding invoice right away.

---

<a name="BeforeYouStart"></a>

## Before You Start

- **Who can manage billing** - Only the account **Owner**, or a **Super Admin** or **Admin** of the main account, can see the billing pages. Other users don't see them.
- **Where to find it** - Click your profile icon at the top right of the portal, then choose **Payment Settings** or **Billing History**. Both are listed under **Billing & Orders** in the settings menu on the left.
- **Sub-accounts and teams with their own billing** - Each has its own billing email and payment method, managed from **Sub-Account Billing** or the team's billing page. See [How to Manage Sub-Account Billing with OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/17129313173395-How-to-manage-Sub-Account-billing-with-OptiSigns).
- **Changing your plan or number of screens** - That's done on the **Subscription Plan** page. See [Billing: Changing Plans or Screen Counts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500000493782).

<a name="UpdateYourBillingEmail"></a>

## Update Your Billing Email

Your billing email is the address on your billing account. It can be different from the email you sign in with, so billing emails can go to your accounts team instead of to you.

Go to **Payment Settings**. In **Billing Email**, click **Update Email**.

![Payment Settings with the Billing Email and Billing Information sections and the Update Email button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616997717779)

Type the new address and click **Save**. You'll see "Your billing email has been saved!".

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| You can enter one billing email address. To send billing emails to several people, use a shared mailbox or a distribution list as your billing email. |

<a name="UpdateYourPaymentMethodAndAddress"></a>

## Update Your Payment Method and Address

### Payment Method

Go to **Payment Settings**. In **Billing Information**, you'll see your payment method on file, or "No payment method on file". Click **Update Payment Method**.

Choose how you want to pay, fill in the details, and click **Save**.

![Billing Information form with Card and US bank account options and card number, expiry, CVC, country and ZIP fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616993119635)

The payment options depend on the currency you pay in:

- **US dollars and Canadian dollars** - Card or US bank account.
- **Euros** - Card, iDEAL, SEPA Direct Debit, Sofort or Bancontact.
- **British pounds and Australian dollars** - Card only.

Sub-accounts and teams with their own billing can pay by card only. For details, see [Accepted Payment Methods](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/34379983350163-Accepted-Payment-Methods).

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| The payment method you save is charged automatically for your future invoices. If you have an unpaid invoice, it's charged as soon as you save. |

### Billing Address

Expand **Billing Address** and click **Update Billing Address**. Fill in **Address**, **City**, **Country**, **State** and **Zip / Postal Code**. **Address Line 2** and **Phone** are optional. Click **Save**.

### Tax ID

If your billing address is outside the US, you can add your VAT number or other tax ID so it appears on your invoices. The **Tax ID** section appears only after you've saved a billing address outside the US.

Expand **Tax ID** and click **Update Tax**. Click **+ Add another ID**, choose the ID type, type your tax ID and click the green tick to save it. To remove an ID, click the red bin next to it. You can add up to 5 tax IDs.

### Coupon Code

Expand **Discount**, enter your coupon code and click **Apply**.

<a name="FixAFailedOrPastDuePayment"></a>

## Fix a Failed or Past-Due Payment

If your bank declines a payment, the invoice stays unpaid until it's settled. Your screens and content stay saved. The quickest fix is to update your payment method: saving a working card pays the outstanding invoice right away.

### How You'll Know a Payment Failed

- **A banner at the top of the portal** - "Payment is past due. Settle it by [date] to keep your account fully active." Click **View invoice** to go to **Billing History**.
- **A reminder when you sign in** - "Payment needed", with the date to pay by. Click **View invoice** to pay, or **Not now** to close it until your next sign-in.
- **On the billing pages** - **Subscription Plan** shows "You have unpaid invoices" with the reason your bank gave, such as insufficient funds or an expired card. **Payment Settings** lists the invoice under **Unpaid Invoices**, and **Billing History** shows it as **Failed Charge**.

![Subscription Plan showing You have unpaid invoices, the reason the bank declined it, and Update Payment Method](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616993120531)

The banner and email reminders start as soon as the payment fails. About a week later, you'll also see the reminder when you sign in. The banner shows the date to pay by, which is two weeks after the payment failed.

### Pay the Outstanding Invoice

Go to **Payment Settings**. Your unpaid invoice is listed at the top, under **Unpaid Invoices**. In **Billing Information**, click **Update Payment Method**.

![Payment Settings with the Unpaid Invoices section listing the declined invoice and the amount due](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616997720595)

Enter a working card or other payment method and click **Save**. OptiSigns charges your outstanding invoices straight away:

- **"Your card was saved and the [amount] payment is complete."** - You'll see this under **Payment Method Updated**. You're all set: the invoice shows **Successfully Charged** in **Billing History**, the banner goes away and your account is fully active again.
- **"Your card was saved, but the payment was not made"** - The new card was declined too. Try a different card, or contact your bank.
- **"Your card was saved, but your bank still needs to confirm the payment"** - Click **Complete payment** to approve it with your bank. See "My Bank Needs to Confirm the Payment" in [Troubleshooting and FAQ](#TroubleshootingAndFAQ).

You can also start from **Billing History**: click **Update Payment** next to the failed invoice to go to **Payment Settings**.

|  |
| --- |
| **TIP** |
| Card about to expire? OptiSigns shows a reminder before your renewal when the card on file expires first. Update it ahead of time and your next payment goes through as usual. |

### If the Invoice Stays Unpaid

While the payment is past due, your existing screens and content keep running, but some actions are paused. You'll see "Please pay your outstanding invoice to add screens and users, or publish content." Paying the invoice lifts these limits.

If the invoice is still unpaid by the date shown, the account is paused and your screens stop showing your content. Nothing is deleted. When you sign in, you'll see "Your subscription needs attention" and "Your content is safe. Please update your payment information to continue."

![Your subscription needs attention dialog with the Update Payment button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616993123859)

Click **Update Payment**, enter a working payment method and click **Save**. Once the invoice is paid, your account becomes active again and your screens pick up your content.

If your subscription has been cancelled, **Update Payment** opens the plans instead. Choose your plan and number of screens to subscribe again. Your content is still there.

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| Only the account Owner, a Super Admin or an Admin can pay. Other users see "Please contact your account admin" or "Already paid? Contact your Super Admin or Account Owner." If that's you, ask one of them to update the payment method. |

If you pay by invoice or purchase order rather than by card, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) about an overdue invoice.

## View Billing History and Download Receipts

Go to **Billing History** under **Billing & Orders** in the settings menu.

![Settings menu with Billing History selected under Billing and Orders](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616993124499)

**Invoice History** lists your upcoming charge and your past invoices, with the **Invoice ID**, **Status**, **Date** and **Amount** of each.

![Invoice History with a Failed Charge row and its Update Payment button highlighted above three Successfully Charged invoices](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616997725971)

The status is one of:

- **Successfully Charged** - The invoice is paid.
- **Failed Charge** - The payment didn't go through. Click **Update Payment** to fix it.
- **Waiting for bank confirmation** - Your bank needs you to approve the payment. Click **Complete payment**.
- **Upcoming Charge** - Your next invoice. Click the view button to see what it will charge.

To get a receipt or an invoice, click the **View receipt** button (the arrow icon) on that invoice. The invoice opens in a new tab. Click **Download invoice** or **Download receipt** to save it as a PDF, or **View invoice and payment details** to see the details first.

![Invoice paid page with the amount, payment date and the Download invoice and Download receipt buttons](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57616993128211)

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common issues our customers may face with billing details, payments and receipts, and how to solve them.

#### My Payment Failed. How Do I Fix It?

Update your payment method in **Payment Settings**. Saving a working card charges the outstanding invoice right away. See [Fix a Failed or Past-Due Payment](#FixAFailedOrPastDuePayment).

#### My Bank Needs to Confirm the Payment

Some banks ask you to approve a payment, for example with a code or in your banking app. Click **Complete payment** on the banner or in **Billing History** to approve it on the secure payment page that opens in a new tab. You haven't been charged until you approve it. Updating your card doesn't complete this check.

#### I Updated My Card but Still See the Past-Due Banner

Refresh the page. If the banner is still there, check **Billing History**: if the invoice still shows **Failed Charge**, the new card was declined too, so try another card or contact your bank. If you've already paid and the banner stays, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).

#### Can I Have My Invoices and Receipts Sent to a Different Email Address?

Yes. Change your **Billing Email** in **Payment Settings**. See [Update Your Billing Email](#UpdateYourBillingEmail).

#### I Don't See the Tax ID Section

It appears only when your billing address is outside the US. Save your billing address first.

#### "You do not have access to this page"

Billing pages are only available to the account Owner, a Super Admin or an Admin. Ask one of them.

#### "Invalid coupon code"

Check the code for typos and make sure it hasn't expired. If it still doesn't work, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).

#### Can I Pay by Invoice or Purchase Order?

Contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) to ask about paying by invoice or purchase order.

#### How Do I Change My Plan or Number of Screens?

See [Billing: Changing Plans or Screen Counts](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500000493782).

#### Do You Offer a Non-Profit or Education Discount?

See [Does OptiSigns Offer a Non-profit/Education Discount?](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016252654-Does-OptiSigns-offer-Non-profit-Education-discount)
