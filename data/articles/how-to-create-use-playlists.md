A playlist plays your images, videos, apps, templates and feeds one after another, in the order you choose, on a continuous loop. Build it once in **Playlists**, then push it to as many screens as you like. Any change you make later reaches every screen playing it.

---

<a name="WhatIsAPlaylist"></a>

## What Is a Playlist

Think of a playlist as a running order for your screens. A clothing store, for example, can rotate seasonal collections, promotions and styling tips in one playlist, so the screen always has something fresh to show.

A playlist can mix any content you have in OptiSigns: uploaded images, videos and documents, apps, templates, feeds, and even other playlists. Each item plays for the time you set, then the next one starts.

<a name="CreateAPlaylist"></a>

## Create a Playlist

Click **Playlists** in the top navigation bar, then click **Create Playlist** above your list of playlists.

![Create Playlist button above the playlist list on the Playlists page](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467677715)

Type a name that's easy to recognize, such as "Mondo Cool Lobby", and click **Create**. The **Create** button stays disabled until you enter a name.

![New Playlist dialog with a playlist name entered](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444672403)

Your new playlist opens in the editor, in the folder you were viewing. To rename it later, click its name at the top of the editor. Next to the name you'll see the number of items, the total file size and the total play time.

![Playlist name at the top of the playlist editor](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467679635)

<a name="AddAndArrangeContent"></a>

## Add and Arrange Content

### Add Content

The panel on the right lists your files, folders and other assets. Drag any asset from that panel into your playlist. You can also click **Drag items here or click to browse assets** below the list.

![Asset panel on the right listing assets to drag into the playlist](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444675603)

To create something new without leaving the page, click **New** at the top of the asset panel and choose **Upload Files**, **Apps**, **Templates** or **Feeds**.

![New menu in the asset panel: Upload Files, Apps, Templates, Feeds](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444676883)

To play one playlist inside another, click **Add Playlist** and choose the playlist to add. Adding playlists inside a playlist is available on paid plans. To learn more, see [How to Create and Use Nested Playlists](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043621953-How-to-create-and-use-Nested-Playlists).

|  |
| --- |
| **IMPORTANT** |
| On the Free plan, a playlist holds up to 30 items and 200 MB of content. Upgrade to a paid plan to add more. |

### Change the Order

Drag items up or down to change the order they play in. To jump an item straight to the start or the end, right-click it and choose **Move to top** or **Move to bottom**.

### Set How Long Each Item Plays

New items play for 20 seconds unless you've set a different default. To change an item's play time, click its duration, type a new number of seconds. The shortest duration is 4 seconds; documents need at least 4 seconds per page.

![Item duration field open for editing in a playlist row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444679571)

If you change the length of a video, a reset button appears next to its duration. Click it to go back to the default.

![Reset duration button next to a changed video duration in a playlist row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444680851)

To change several items at once, hover over the item numbers and tick the checkboxes. Click **Edit Duration** to set one duration for all of them, or **Remove** to take them out of the playlist.

![One playlist item selected, showing Edit Duration and Remove](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467682707)

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| Some content controls its own timing, so its duration can't be edited. This includes MRSS feeds, content from Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive, and Content Rotation apps. |

### Item Options

Click the gear icon on an item to open **Asset Options**. Here you can change how that one item plays:

- **Transition** - The effect used when this item appears. Leave it on **Use Playlist Default** to follow the playlist's setting.
- **Target tags** - Play the item only on screens with matching tags.
- **Item Schedule** - Attach a schedule so the item plays only at certain times.
- **Play Every** - Play the item only once in a set period, such as every 15 minutes, instead of on every loop.
- **Mute** - Silence the sound of a video item. This option appears for content with audio.

Click **Save** to apply your changes.

![Asset Options dialog with transition, target tags, item schedule and play every](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444682387)

<a name="PlaylistSettingsAndActions"></a>

## Playlist Settings and Actions

### Playlist Options

To change how the whole playlist plays, click the **More actions** menu at the top of the playlist and choose **Playlist Options**. You can set how images, videos and documents are scaled on screen, turn on **Shuffle**, pick the **Default Transition** and its speed, and add **Background Music**. To learn more, see [How to Manage and Edit Your Playlist Settings](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28295074995859-How-to-Manage-and-Edit-Your-Playlist-Settings).

![Playlist Options dialog with scale, shuffle and transition settings](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444683667)

### Default Playlist Settings

Default settings apply to every playlist in your account. Click **Playlist Settings** at the top of the playlist to open **Default Playlist Settings**. Set the **Default item duration**, the default image, video and document scale, the **Default transition** and the default **Target tags**, then click **Save**. New items you add use these defaults.

![Default Playlist Settings dialog](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467686163)

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| Only the account owner or a super admin can change the default playlist settings. |

### Saving Your Changes

There's no Save button to remember. Your playlist saves automatically a few seconds after each change, and the top of the editor shows **Changes Saved**. Use **Undo** and **Redo** at the top of the playlist to step back through recent edits, and **Preview** to watch the playlist play.

![Changes Saved status next to the playlist name in the editor](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467687827)

### More Actions

Click the **More actions** menu (three dots) at the top of the playlist for everything else you can do with it:

- **Playlist Options** - Change how the whole playlist plays.
- **Move Playlist** - Move it into another folder.
- **Duplicate Playlist** - Create a copy of the playlist.
- **Share Link** - Share the playlist with someone through a link.
- **Playlist Operation Report** - See a report of activity on the playlist.
- **Used In** - See which screens, schedules and split screens use this playlist.
- **Move to Trash** - Delete the playlist. You can restore it from **Trash** for 30 days.

![Playlist more-actions menu](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444684563)

<a name="PushYourPlaylistToScreens"></a>

## Push Your Playlist to Screens

When your playlist is ready, click **Push to Screens** at the top of the playlist.

![Push to Screens button in the playlist toolbar](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467690899)

Under **Screens**, choose the screens that should play it. Then choose when it plays:

- **Push Now** - Plays the playlist on the selected screens right away.
- **Schedule** - Starts the playlist at the **Go-Live Date & Time** you choose. Tick **Set expiration time** to also choose when it stops.
- **Temporarily** - Takes over the screens for a set time, such as 5 minutes or 1 hour, then goes back to what they were playing before.

![Push To Screens dialog on the Push Now tab](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467692947)

Click **Push** to confirm. The button reads **Schedule Push** or **Push Temporarily** when you choose those options.

![Push To Screens dialog on the Temporarily tab with takeover durations](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909444688147)

### Assign a Playlist From the Screens Page

You can also set a playlist from the screen itself. Go to **Screens**, click **Edit** on the screen, and set **Content Type** to **Playlist**. Choose your playlist in the field below it, then click **Save**.

![Edit Screen dialog Content Type list: Asset, Playlist, Schedule, Stop Playing](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57909467695379)

To learn more, see [Push Contents to Your Screens](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18988049363859-Push-Contents-to-your-Screens).

<a name="TroubleshootingAndFAQ"></a>

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common issues our customers may face when using playlists, and how to solve them.

#### Why can't I create a playlist?

You can only create playlists from **Home**. If you're viewing **Shared**, **Favorites** or **Trash**, switch to **Home** first, then click **Create Playlist**.

#### Why can't I add more items to my playlist?

On the Free plan, each playlist holds up to 30 items and 200 MB. Remove items you no longer need, or upgrade to a paid plan for larger playlists.

#### Why does Add Playlist ask me to upgrade?

Adding a playlist inside another playlist is a paid-plan feature. Upgrade your plan to use nested playlists.

#### Why can't I change an item's duration?

Some content sets its own timing, such as MRSS feeds, content from Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive, and Content Rotation apps. Their duration can't be edited in the playlist.

#### Why can't I change the default playlist settings?

Only the account owner or a super admin can change **Default Playlist Settings**. Ask one of them to make the change.

#### My changes didn't save. What should I do?

If saving fails, the top of the editor shows **Error** with a **Retry** button. Click **Retry**. OptiSigns also retries a failed save automatically.

#### I deleted a playlist by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Open **Trash** in the playlist list and restore it. Deleted playlists stay in Trash for 30 days.

#### How do I find out which screens use a playlist?

Open the playlist, click the **More actions** menu and choose **Used In**. It lists the screens, schedules and split screens that use it.
