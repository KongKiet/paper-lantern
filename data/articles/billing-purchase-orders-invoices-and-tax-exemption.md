<a name="GetAQuoteAndPayByPurchaseOrder"></a>

## Get a Quote and Pay by Purchase Order

The portal doesn't create quotes or purchase orders. To get a quote, email our billing team at [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) with:

- **Plan** - The plan you want, such as Standard, Pro Plus or Engage. See our [pricing page](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing) to compare them.
- **Number of screens** - How many screen licenses you need.
- **Billing address** - Your organization's name and billing address.
- **Devices** - Optional. Any OptiSigns devices you'd like to order, how many, and the shipping address.

Our billing team will send you a quote. Once your procurement department has issued a purchase order, send the PO, or just the PO number, to [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com). If your organization doesn't use purchase orders, let us know and we'll go ahead without one.

We'll then email you an invoice with your PO number on it. The invoice email has a link to pay online, and it shows the date the payment is due.

<a name="PayByBankAccountOrCheck"></a>

## Pay by Bank Account or Check

- **US bank account** - If your account is billed in US dollars, you can pay from a US bank account instead of a card. Add it under **Payment Settings**; see [Update Your Payment Method and Address](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014#UpdateYourPaymentMethodAndAddress). This option isn't available in other currencies.
- **Check** - We accept checks in US dollars only, with a $25 payment processing fee.

<a name="SalesTaxAndVAT"></a>

## Sales Tax and VAT

Sales tax is calculated automatically from your billing address, so make sure the address in **Payment Settings** is complete and correct.

When you subscribe or change your subscription, the **Due Today** amount already includes any tax. The tax appears as its own line on your payment confirmation and on your invoice.

![Your plan is updated confirmation with the subtotal, a Sales Tax - Texas (8.25%) line and the total](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57730832146963)

If your billing address is outside the US, you can add your VAT number or other tax ID so it appears on your invoices. See [Tax ID](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041587014#TaxID).

![Tax ID section in Payment Settings with the Update Tax button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57730820312211)

<a name="TaxExemption"></a>

## Tax Exemption

If your organization is exempt from sales tax, email a copy of your exemption certificate to [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com). We accept documents such as a 501(c)(3) determination letter or a state sales and use tax exemption certificate.

Once our billing team has approved it, your future invoices won't include sales tax.

- **Tax you've already paid** - Sales tax charged before your exemption was approved is returned to you as account credit, which is used toward your next invoices.
- **What's covered** - The exemption applies to your subscription and to devices you order in the OptiSigns portal. It doesn't apply to devices bought from shop.optisigns.com or Amazon.

<a name="TroubleshootingAndFAQ"></a>

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

Here are a few of the more common questions our customers ask about purchase orders, invoices and tax, and how to solve them.

#### Can I Get a Quote From the Portal?

No. Quotes come from our billing team. Email [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com) with your plan, number of screens and billing address.

#### My Invoice Is Overdue. What Should I Do?

Pay it from the link in the invoice email. If you can't find the email, or you've already paid, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).

#### Why Was I Charged Sales Tax?

Sales tax is based on your billing address. Check that the address in **Payment Settings** is correct. If your organization is tax-exempt, send us your certificate as described in [Tax Exemption](#TaxExemption).

#### I Don't See the Tax ID Section

The **Tax ID** section only appears once you've saved a billing address outside the US. US customers don't need one.

#### Can I Pay by Bank Account in Canadian Dollars or Another Currency?

Paying from a bank account in the portal is available in US dollars only. Accounts billed in euros have other options, such as SEPA Direct Debit and iDEAL. For anything else, contact [billing@optisigns.com](mailto:billing@optisigns.com).
