OptiSigns has four AI tools. Each one does a different job: getting help, designing content, building apps, or managing your screens from the AI assistant you already use. You choose which ones to use, and you can turn them off.

---

<a name="AIToolsAtAGlance"></a>

## AI Tools at a Glance

- **OptiBot** - An AI assistant built into the portal. Ask it how to do something, troubleshoot a screen, or have it make changes for you.
- **AI Designer** - Describe the sign you want, and it creates ready-to-edit designs in under a minute.
- **OptiDev** - Describe an app, kiosk or interactive display in plain words, and OptiDev builds it for your screens.
- **AI Connector** - Lets ChatGPT, Claude or Grok manage your OptiSigns content, playlists, schedules and screens for you.

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| No AI tool changes your account without asking you first. OptiBot and the AI Connector both ask for your approval before they change, delete or replace anything. |

## OptiBot: Your AI Assistant in the Portal

OptiBot answers questions using the OptiSigns Help Center, looks things up in your account, and can do tasks for you. For example, it can upload content, build a playlist, set up a schedule, or open a support ticket.

To start a chat, click the OptiBot button (**Ask OptiBot anything**) in the portal. The OptiBot panel opens beside the page you're working on.

![The OptiBot button in the bottom-right corner of the portal, with its Ask OptiBot anything tooltip](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688510385043)

![The OptiBot panel open with its welcome message and the Ask me anything box highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688520358547)

To get help with a specific screen, go to **Screens**, click the **...** menu on that screen, then click **Troubleshoot with OptiBot**. OptiBot checks the screen's status and walks you through the fix.

![A screen's More menu on the Screens page with Troubleshoot with OptiBot highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688505242643)

Whenever OptiBot wants to change something, it shows an **Action requires your approval** card. Nothing happens until you click **Approve**. Click **Deny** to cancel.

What OptiBot can do depends on your role. Users with **Read Only** or **Content Proposal** roles can ask questions and look things up, but OptiBot won't make changes for them. Only the **Account Owner** and **Super Admins** can use OptiBot to send commands to a device.

## AI Designer: Create Designs from a Description

AI Designer turns a short description into finished sign designs that you can edit in the Designer.

To open it, go to **Templates** and click **Try AI Designer**.

![The Templates page with the Try AI Designer button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688528537491)

Type your idea in the **Describe your idea...** box. For example: *"Weekly lunch specials for a cafe, warm colors, space for three dishes and prices."* To match your brand or a photo, click the image button next to the box to add up to six reference images. Then pick an orientation from the menu, which starts on **Landscape**, and click the arrow button.

![The OptiSigns AI Designer prompt box with a sample description typed in and the generate arrow button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688543073811)

AI Designer creates your designs in under 30 seconds. Pick the one you like to open it in the Designer, where you can change any text, image or color before you add it to a playlist. For ideas to start from, see **Explore AI Prompts**.

|  |
| --- |
| **TIP** |
| AI can make mistakes. Check names, prices, dates and spelling before a design goes on screen. |

You can also ask OptiBot to make a design. It creates three versions and gives you an **Open in Designer** button.

The Designer's **Background Remover** also uses AI. It removes an image's background right in your web browser, so the image never leaves your computer. To learn more, see [Explore Designer 2.0 New Features](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/41432385864595).

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| AI designs open in Designer 2.0 or the New Designer. If your account still uses the classic Designer, the portal asks you to switch to Designer 2.0 first. |

## OptiDev: Build Apps and Interactive Content with AI

OptiDev builds the content that templates can't, such as interactive kiosks, custom dashboards, and apps that pull in live data. Describe what you need in plain words, watch OptiDev build it, then push it to your screens.

Open OptiDev from the **OptiDev** tab at the top of the portal. The tab appears once your account has at least one paired screen.

![The portal's top navigation with the OptiDev tab highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688520988947)

OptiDev is an add-on with a 14-day free trial, and the trial includes monthly AI credits. An account owner or admin activates it. Other users see a message asking them to contact an admin. To show a finished project on a screen, add the **OptiDev** app to a playlist and select your project.

For trial details, AI credits and pricing, see [OptiDev Add-on - Free Trial and Pricing](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/54095075680275).

## AI Connector: Manage OptiSigns from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok

With the AI Connector, you can manage OptiSigns by chatting with the AI assistant you already use. Ask ChatGPT, Claude or Grok to upload a flyer, build a playlist, set a schedule, or check which screens are offline, and it does the work in your account.

The AI Connector is available on every plan at no extra cost. Your assistant signs in as you, so it can only do what your OptiSigns role allows. It asks before it deletes anything or changes what a screen is playing, and deleted items go to the Trash.

The connector is turned on and off for the whole account in **Settings** > **Preferences**, in the **General** section, with the **Enable AI connector (MCP)** checkbox. Only the **Account Owner** or a **Super Admin** can change it.

![Preferences, General section, with the Enable AI connector (MCP) checkbox highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57688511186451)

To add OptiSigns to your assistant and sign in, see [Connect ChatGPT, Claude and Grok to OptiSigns Digital Signage with AI Connector](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/56987330525587-Connect-ChatGPT-Claude-and-Grok-to-OptiSigns-Digital-Signage-with-AI-Connector).

## Your Data and Turning AI Off

### Does OptiSigns Use Our Content to Train AI?

No. OptiSigns does not use your content, designs, files or account data to train AI models.

### Is Our Data Sent to AI Companies?

Only when you use an AI tool, and only what that request needs. For example, when you describe a design, AI Designer sends your description and any reference images to the AI service that creates the design. When you ask OptiBot a question, it sends your question and the account details needed to answer it. That data is used to give you an answer. It is not used to train AI models.

If you don't use an AI tool, it doesn't send your data anywhere.

The AI Connector works a little differently. You connect your own ChatGPT, Claude or Grok account, so the information your assistant reads from OptiSigns is handled under your agreement with that AI company.

For the full list of service providers and how OptiSigns handles AI data, see the [OptiSigns Trust Center](https://www.optisigns.com/trust-center#ai-usage).

### How Do I Turn AI Off?

There isn't a single switch that turns off every AI tool. Each tool is controlled on its own:

- **AI Connector** - The Account Owner or a Super Admin unchecks **Enable AI connector (MCP)** in **Settings** > **Preferences** > **General**, then clicks **Save Changes**. Every connected assistant stops working right away. To disconnect only your own assistant, remove OptiSigns from that assistant's settings.
- **OptiDev** - OptiDev is off unless you activate it. To stop using it, go to **Account Add-Ons** on your **Subscription Plan** page and cancel the OptiDev add-on or its trial.
- **AI Designer** - AI Designer only runs when someone clicks **Try AI Designer**. If you don't use it, nothing is sent.
- **OptiBot** - OptiBot can't be turned off, but it only answers questions until you approve an action. To limit what a user can do, give them a **Read Only** or **Content Proposal** role. OptiBot then answers their questions but never makes changes.

### Is AI Analytics an AI Tool?

No. In **AI Analytics**, the **AI Add-on** and the **AI Camera** app, "AI" stands for **Audience Intelligence**. It counts viewers anonymously on the device itself and isn't one of the AI tools in this article. To learn more, see [AI Camera FAQs](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058259834).
